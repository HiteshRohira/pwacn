# Interaction vocabulary

Use these terms in review notes so different runs remain comparable.

- **Contact response:** visible acknowledgement after pointer contact, before activation.
- **Directness:** how closely the surface follows the pointer while ownership is active.
- **Tracking error:** pointer displacement minus surface displacement on the active axis.
- **Velocity continuity:** how much release velocity is carried into the first settling frame.
- **Commit:** the semantic action that becomes durable at release or threshold crossing.
- **Cancel:** return to the prior semantic state without activating the action.
- **Interruption:** a new gesture seizing a surface before its existing animation completes.
- **Settle:** motion after release until the surface reaches a stable resting state.
- **Handoff:** gesture ownership transferring between scroll, sheet, navigation, or another
  recognizer without a discontinuity.
- **Turnaround:** the moment pointer direction reverses during one gesture.

Qualitative judgments should name the moment and evidence: for example, “turnaround feels
heavy because the surface trails the pointer by two frames,” not simply “animation is slow.”
